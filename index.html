How the scoring works

Price score = lowest entered price ÷ supplier price × 100. Delivery score = fastest entered delivery ÷ supplier delivery × 100. Quality is your entered score out of 100. Each is multiplied by its normalized weight and added to produce the total. All valid quotes, including late quotes, determine the price and delivery baselines. Equal totals share a rank.

Quality scores are supplied by you, not independently verified. Results support a purchasing decision; they do not place orders or connect to SAP. This page does not save quotations after you close or reload it.
